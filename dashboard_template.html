Driver against car

Each dot is a driver: across, his team's pace (Equipment); up, his own (Rating). Above the line he out-ran the car, below it the car carried him. 50 is the season average, 15 one standard deviation.

Driver ratings

Rating: the driver's own green-flag pace with his team's share taken out. Equip.: the team's share. Pace: his plain green-flag pace. Driver vs car is Rating minus Equip. The split uses the teammates he raced beside, so a driver alone on his team, or on a team that rotates drivers, is only roughly separated from his car. Laps are green-flag laps; lap times exist from 2020. See ratings.py.
